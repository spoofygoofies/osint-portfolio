# OSINT portfolio — spoofygoofies

Open-source investigations, written up with method, sources and confidence levels.

| Case | Date | Methods |
|---|---|---|
| [Fire at the NZNP oil terminal, Rostov-on-Don](rostov-oil-terminal-2026-10/) | 9–13 Oct 2026 | Telegram collection with chain of custody, geolocation, NASA FIRMS, Sentinel-2, company registers, sanctions data, competing hypotheses |

Collection is passive only. No personal data of filmers or residents is published; camera positions in residential areas are given at district level.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [tg-evidence-collector](https://github.com/spoofygoofies/tg-evidence-collector) | Collects public Telegram posts with original media, SHA-256 hashes, UTC times and Wayback Machine copies (chain of custody) |
